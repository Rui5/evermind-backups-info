Privacy policy

How this personal-use backup integration handles your data.

Last updated · 27 October 2026

1. Scope and operator

Evermind Backups is a personal-use integration operated by the owner of a private household’s Evermind installation. It uses Google Drive as a destination for locally encrypted backups. This policy covers the Google Drive backup integration and this information website. It does not authorize other Evermind features to send information to cloud AI providers.

The integration is currently being validated with synthetic data. Complete application recovery and automated production backups are not yet enabled.

2. Data accessed

After you authorize the app, it uses Google OAuth credentials and access tokens to call the Google Drive API. It may access:

  • Your authorized account’s email address and storage-quota information, to verify the backup account and available capacity.
  • Drive files and folders created by the app or explicitly authorized for it, including their content, names, identifiers, sizes, timestamps, and permissions.
  • Encrypted backup objects and the metadata needed to create, check, manage, and restore a backup repository.

The requested scope is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. It permits creating and managing app-authorized files; it does not grant general access to every existing Drive file. The integration does not request access to Gmail, Google Contacts, or Google Calendar.

3. Use and sharing

Google user data is used to authenticate the connection, verify the selected account, check storage and folder access, and perform owner-authorized backup and recovery operations. Changes to existing backup contents or retention must follow the owner’s approved configuration.

The backup integration does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or send it to AI model providers. Data obtained through the Google Drive API is not used to train AI models. Google user data is not transferred to other services by this backup workflow.

Google receives encrypted backup objects and associated storage and account metadata. Cloud storage remains subject to Google’s own policies. Backup folders are intended for the authorized owner; the app does not create public sharing links or grant other users access. Local computer administrators and anyone holding the repository and recovery password may be able to access recovered contents.

4. Storage and security

Canonical Evermind data stays on the owner’s local computer. The backup workflow uses restic to encrypt backup contents locally before rclone transfers them to Google Drive over HTTPS. Recovery requires the repository’s password. Encryption does not conceal the Google account, encrypted-object sizes, or storage timestamps from Google.

OAuth client credentials, access and refresh tokens, and backup passwords are stored outside the source-code repository in local files restricted by filesystem permissions. These file permissions do not protect secrets from the computer’s administrator or a compromised operating system. The rclone configuration is not itself encrypted by this setup.

The owner is responsible for controlling access to the computer and Drive account, keeping an independent recovery password, and verifying complete recovery. The completed synthetic recovery test does not establish that a full production installation can yet be recovered.

5. Retention and deletion

No automatic production retention schedule is currently enabled. Local credentials remain until the operator removes them or replaces the setup. Encrypted cloud backup objects and snapshots remain until the operator deletes them under an approved retention or removal process.

Deleting a source record or disconnecting Google does not automatically erase copies in earlier backup snapshots. Removing those copies requires a separate backup-retention or deletion operation. Google’s trash, retention, and account policies can affect when deleted cloud objects are finally removed. Restored application state must also respect current deletions and revoked access before use.

6. Your control

You can review or revoke the connection through Google Account connections. Revocation stops the app from continuing authorized Google Drive access until you authorize it again; it does not by itself delete existing backups or local credentials.

To remove local credentials, change backup settings, request removal of stored backup copies, or ask about your information, contact the household’s project operator. Each person must approve the backup and sharing of their own private information before it is included. Authorizing the Google account alone does not authorize importing every household member’s data.

7. This information website

These static pages have no sign-in, upload forms, cookies, analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts added by the project. They do not contain OAuth credentials or backup data. Links to Google open services operated under Google’s policies.

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process network request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, under its privacy statement. The project does not add analytics or tracking scripts.

8. Changes and contact

This policy must be updated when the integration’s data practices change. Changes that require additional access or a new use of Google user data require disclosure and fresh authorization before that use begins.

For privacy questions or removal requests, contact your household’s project operator using the support email displayed on the Google consent screen. The date above identifies the current published version of this policy.

Reference: Google API Services User Data Policy and Google Drive permission scopes.
